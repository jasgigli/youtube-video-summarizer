## Executive Summary
Latin America's Itaú Unibanco has implemented an 'AI First' strategy, deploying generative AI to its entire customer base and workforce. Utilizing the conversational interface ia.i and the IARA governance platform, the institution successfully scales AI in one of the region's most heavily regulated sectors.
